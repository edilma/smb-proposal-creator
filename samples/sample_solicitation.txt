REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS — City of Testville Website Redesign (RFP-2026-TEST)

The City of Testville invites proposals for the redesign of its public website.

Proposals are due November 30, 2026 at 2:00 PM Eastern Time.

The selected vendor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits
of not less than $5,000,000 in the aggregate.

Vendors must have a minimum of five (5) years in business.

The vendor must be registered to do business in the State of Florida.
